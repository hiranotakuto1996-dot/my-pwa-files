Privacy Policy

GeoFine Alarm (hereinafter referred to as the "App") recognizes the importance of protecting personal information, respects users' privacy, and complies with the Act on the Protection of Personal Information and other applicable laws and regulations. This Privacy Policy sets forth how the App collects, uses, and manages user information.

1. Information We Collect

The App may collect the following information for the purpose of providing and operating the service:

  • Email address and other information necessary for authentication
  • Country or region of residence
  • Device identifiers, authentication tokens, and other device/account-related information
  • Alarm settings and alarm history
  • Payment history and information related to rewards
  • Usage information for fraud prevention and security purposes

2. Collection of Location Information

The App obtains location information from the user's device for the purpose of alarm judgment and notification functions. However, such location information is, in principle, used only within the user's device and is not stored on the App provider's servers (including Firestore).

Users may manage whether to allow the App to access location information through their device settings. If users do not permit the use of location information, certain features may not be available.

3. Purpose of Use

The App uses the collected information only for the following purposes:

  • User authentication and account management
  • Provision of alarm and notification functions
  • Operation and management of reward features
  • Payment processing and usage management
  • Prevention of fraudulent activities and ensuring security
  • Service improvement and quality enhancement

4. Storage and Management of Information

The App may store collected information on cloud servers managed by the App provider (such as Firebase / Google Cloud).

Such information is managed under appropriate access controls and security measures, and no third party other than the App provider may view or obtain such information without authorization.

As described in the previous section, location information is, in principle, not stored on the App provider's servers.

5. Provision to Third Parties and Outsourcing

The App will not provide personal information to third parties except in the following cases:

  • When the user's consent has been obtained
  • When disclosure is required by laws and regulations
  • When it is necessary to provide information to contractors within the scope necessary for payment processing, authentication processing, and server operation

The App may use the following external services:

  • Firebase (Google LLC)
  • Apple App Store / Google Play (payment and distribution platforms)

6. Disclosure, Correction, and Deletion of Personal Information

Users may request disclosure, correction, or deletion of their personal information by contacting the App provider through the prescribed method.

However, information that is required to be retained by law or that is necessary for the provision of the service may not be deleted immediately.

7. Use by Minors

The App is, in principle, intended for users who are 18 years of age or older.

By using the App, users confirm that they are at least 18 years old. The use of the App by persons under the age of 18 is not contemplated.

If the App provider becomes aware that a person under the age of 18 is using the App, the provider may suspend or terminate the relevant account and take other necessary measures. In addition, with respect to any rewards granted or received in connection with such use, the provider may invalidate such rewards, request their return, or request repayment of the equivalent value of rewards already distributed.

8. Security Measures

The App implements reasonable security measures to prevent leakage, loss, or alteration of the information it collects.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be changed without prior notice due to changes in laws, addition of features, or specification changes.

The revised Privacy Policy shall take effect at the time it is posted within the App or on related pages.
