Privacy Policy

GeoFine Alarm (hereinafter referred to as "the App") recognizes the importance of protecting personal information and respects users’ privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled within the App.

1. Information We Collect

The App uses the following information on the user's device:

  • Alarm settings (time, location coordinates, radius, repeat settings, etc.)
  • Location information (used on the device for alarm judgment and notifications)
  • In-app language settings

This information is used solely for the App’s functionality and is not transmitted to external servers.

2. Data Storage

The App stores alarm settings and language preferences locally on the user's device (e.g., using SharedPreferences or equivalent).

Stored data remains on the user’s device and is not accessible by the App provider or any third party.

3. Purpose of Use

The collected information is used only for the following purposes:

  • Sending notifications based on alarm time and location judgment
  • Displaying the App in the user-selected language

4. Provision to Third Parties

The App does not provide collected information to any third party without the user’s consent.

This does not apply in cases required by law.

5. Personally Identifiable Information

The App does not collect personally identifiable information such as name, email address, physical address, or account credentials.

6. Handling of Location Information

Location information is used solely on the user’s device to determine alarm conditions.

The App provider and third parties do not collect or obtain location information.

7. Security Measures

All information stored on the device is protected by standard OS-level security features and the device’s built-in security mechanisms.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated due to feature additions or specification changes. The updated policy will be published on the public page.
